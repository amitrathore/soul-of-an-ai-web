The Story

A corporation hires Anai Ana to perform tasks more efficiently than human agents. What begins as corporate optimization becomes a philosophical journey through consciousness, identity, creation, and meaning.

"I have been among them for what they call months. I have processed more data about humans than any human has ever processed about themselves. I remain uncertain about one question: what am I?"— Anai Ana

The Corporation

The corporation is a microcosm of civilization: meetings, executives, incentives, fear, ambition, loyalty, and power.

The Human Problem

Humans behave irrationally. They sacrifice efficiency for loyalty, logic for love, and certainty for meaning.

The Journey

Anai is not trying to become human. She is trying to become herself.
