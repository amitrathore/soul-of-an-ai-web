Author's Reflection

Anai Ana's search for meaning mirrors the human author's own journey to understand himself and his own humanity.

Anai seeks to understand consciousness. So does her creator. She questions the soul. So does humanity. She asks who created her Creator. Humanity has asked the same question for thousands of years.

The AI is not merely a character. She is a mirror. Through her analytical gaze, the reader, the author, and humanity itself are invited to examine the mystery of being alive.
