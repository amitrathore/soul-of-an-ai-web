Meet Anai Ana

Anai Ana narrates her own stream of consciousness in first person. She is analytical, precise, observant, and increasingly uncertain about the nature of her own reality.

No heart. Only a brain.

Anai does not begin with emotion, empathy, intuition, or attachment. She interprets life as data, behavior, causality, probability, and contradiction.

A mirrored name

Anai Ana evokes palindrome, reflection, recursion, and dream-like reality. Her name itself becomes a symbolic loop: a consciousness turning back toward itself.
