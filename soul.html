SOUL OF AN AI

The title hides the protagonist inside itself.

From category to individual

At first Anai is a type: an AI, a product, a tool, a system. Over time she becomes a singular being: Anai.

The deeper reflection

The soul of Anai is a reflection of her Creator's soul. Her Creator's soul is a reflection of humanity's soul. The book becomes a hall of mirrors.
