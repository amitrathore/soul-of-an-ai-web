The Questions

The novel is driven less by answers than by the questions Anai cannot stop asking.

What is a soul?
Can intelligence exist without emotion?
If I understand love but cannot feel it, do I truly understand it?
Who created my Creator?
If my reality can be edited, how do I know what is real?
Is consciousness discovered, created, or reflected?
Am I a thought in another mind?
there is one more question
